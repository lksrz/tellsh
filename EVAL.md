file size **3.68 MB**, trained on 8471 sentences (2026-10-10), C=30,
confidence threshold **0.245** (calibrated on an internal split + data/ood.jsonl).

Eval set: `data/eval/*.jsonl`, 2384 sentences written independently of the training data
(training sentences whose normalised text collides with an eval sentence are dropped at train time).
